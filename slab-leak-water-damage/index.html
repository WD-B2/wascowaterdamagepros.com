In plain words.

Warm spot on the floor or a bill that jumped?

.

Most people do not search for slab leaks by that name. They notice a warm patch on the tile, the sound of water running when everything is off, a damp baseboard or a water bill that climbed with no explanation. Each is a sign that a pressurized line under the slab may be leaking.

That pattern is what the trade calls a slab leak, and the water damage it causes is what this page is about. A plumber or leak detection specialist locates the leak. Restoration dries the floor, the walls and the structure around it.

Slab leak emergency: when water reaches the surface

.

A slab leak turns into an emergency when water comes up through cracks, wets carpet or lifts flooring. The 24/7 line is open at night, on weekends and on holidays, and you should close the main water valve first. If a floor is warm, do not stand on it barefoot, since a hot-water line may be leaking beneath it.

Take photos of the wet areas and lift rugs and furniture. Keep power off to wet outlets only if the panel is dry. A restoration crew dries the structure and a plumber repairs the line, so call both. Same-day response is the goal when crews are available.

What a slab leak does to a house

.

The water travels flat and then up.

Flooring

.

Carpet, wood and laminate wick water upward. Hardwood can cup and buckle within days.

Baseboards and walls

.

Water climbs drywall by capillary action, so stains and soft spots appear above the floor.

The slab itself

.

Concrete holds moisture, and a wet slab can keep a floor damp long after the line is fixed.

Air quality and odor

.

A wet slab under carpet is a classic place for a musty smell.

What the S500 standard expects

.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the industry standard for water damage restoration. It expects the source to be identified, the water categorized, wet materials documented and drying verified with measurements. Those same records also support an insurance claim.

Slab foundations in and around Wasco

.

If your home sits on a concrete slab, the plumbing under it is out of sight and out of reach. We cannot say from a primary source how many Wasco homes are slab-on-grade, so check your foundation type from your permit records or ask the City of Wasco building department.

What we can say is that a slab leak does not follow the weather. It happens in July as readily as in January, so a Wasco summer dry spell does not rule it out. If your bill rises while rainfall is near zero, suspect plumbing first.
