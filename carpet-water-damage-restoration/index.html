Carpet water damage: the pad is the problem

.

Carpet water damage is damage to the carpet, the pad beneath and the subfloor after water soaks in. The carpet itself dries on top while the pad holds water against the floor, so a carpet that feels dry can still be wet below.

This is not always a night call, but the 24/7 line is open if water is spreading. Do not walk on soaked carpet more than you have to, and do not run a household vacuum that is not rated for water.

.

Water category and time decide.

Clean water, caught quickly

.

Carpet and pad can often be extracted and dried in place.

Clean water, left for days

.

The pad usually has to go, and mold risk rises.

Gray water

.

Carpet may be cleaned in some cases, the pad is usually removed.

Sewage or flood water

.

Carpet and pad are removed. See flood damage cleanup.

A pet accident or roof leak is not a flood

.

A small wet spot from a leaking air conditioner or a window can look minor. If the spot is large or keeps recurring, the pad may be soaked. A moisture meter on the carpet and the baseboards shows whether the wet area is bigger than it looks.

For wet carpet near the walls, the drywall behind the baseboard is the next place to check. See wall and drywall water damage repair.

What to do while carpet is wet

.

Small actions protect the carpet and the floor.

  • Lift furniture off the carpet and place foil or wood blocks under the legs.

Why the pad matters so much

.

Carpet pad is a foam or fiber layer that holds water like a sponge. Dry carpet on top of wet pad traps moisture against the subfloor, which is where mold and odor begin. Pad is inexpensive to replace compared with the cost of an odor problem or mold.

If the carpet is saved but the pad is removed, the carpet is re-laid on a new pad. A technician checks the tack strips too, since they can rust and stain.

Stains, odor and what drying cannot fix

.

Dye transfer, browning from wicking tannins and permanent stains can remain even when the carpet is dried. These are cosmetic. Odor is different, and persistent odor means moisture remains somewhere. Ask for readings, not just a feel test.

Industry standard to ask about

.

Professional water damage work is guided by the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, which ties the plan to the category of water and the materials involved. A company that follows it can show you moisture readings from the first day and again when the structure is dry.

Carpet in Wasco homes

.

Many valley homes are carpeted over a concrete slab or a plywood subfloor, and slabs keep moisture long after the surface is dry. Check the carpet near exterior walls and under windows after winter rain.
