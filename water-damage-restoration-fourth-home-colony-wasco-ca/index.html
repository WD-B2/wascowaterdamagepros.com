Where the Fourth Home Colony name comes from

.

Wasco was settled as a colony in 1907, when the Fourth Home Extension Colony bought nine sections of land from the Kern County Land Company, according to the Kern Council of Governments. A plat map titled Map of the townsite of Wasco in the Fourth Home Extension Colony is held in the Yale University Library collection, and text attributed to the City of Wasco's website describes the same colony. Water was a founding concern, and the colony set up the Fourth Extension Water Company to supply domestic and irrigation water.

The name did not stay in the history books. County and listing records still tag many Wasco lots as Fourth Home Colony or Fourth Home Ext Colo, from a home on 4th Place near Broadway to houses on Garden Street and Bautista Avenue. Because the label covers so much ground, we treat it as a historic area of Wasco, not as a fenced neighborhood with a hard edge. If your address sits in the original townsite, this page was written for you, and if you are unsure, the dispatcher can sort it out in one question.

What the colony label tells you about the house

.

Listing data for Fourth Home Colony addresses shows no single building era, so the age of your own house matters more than the name.

Homes from the 1960s

.

Listings on Garden Street show houses built in the early 1960s. Homes that old may have original supply lines, aging water heaters and older drain lines, so a plumber can tell you what is actually behind your walls.

Newer tract homes

.

Listings on Bautista Avenue show houses built in 2007 and later. Homes this new are less likely to have aged pipes, so failed appliance hoses, water heaters and AC condensate drains are more often the source.

Small older homes

.

A listing on 4th Place near Broadway shows a one-story home of about 570 square feet. With little floor area, water from a failed line reaches most of the house quickly.

Water in Wasco: what is documented and what is not

.

Wasco averages roughly 6.8 inches of rain a year, mostly from December through March, according to NOAA climate normals. In March 2023, KERO Channel 23 reported flooding in the Wasco area with evacuation orders for the Wasco and McFarland communities. That reporting covers the wider area and does not single out the Fourth Home Colony area, so we do not claim it flooded.

Services Wasco homeowners ask about most

.

Calls usually start with emergency water extraction when water is standing. A failed supply line leads to burst pipe cleanup, and a leaking tank leads to water heater leak cleanup. In newer homes, a clogged condensate line often points to AC leak water damage.

Once the water is out, structural drying brings the materials back to normal moisture, and any visible mold growth belongs with mold remediation.

Insurance paperwork and mold cautions

.

Take photos and short video of the damage before anything is moved, keep damaged items until the insurer has seen them, and write down when you first noticed the water. Coverage depends on your policy and on the cause, so ask your insurer what applies. We cannot promise that a claim will be approved or what it will pay.

Mold can follow water that stays in drywall, carpet pad or framing. If you see or smell growth, avoid disturbing it, keep fans from blowing across it, and ask for an inspection. For other parts of the city and nearby towns, see the full service area page.
