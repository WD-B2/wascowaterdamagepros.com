Water damage rarely arrives alone. A broken supply line can soak a wall, a floor and a ceiling below, and the same crew should handle extraction, drying and repair planning. The pages below explain each type of loss, what the first hours look like and what drying involves.

Professional water damage work is guided by the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, and mold work by S520. Services are grouped by where the problem starts. If you are unsure which one fits, call (844) 950-6900 and describe what you see. The dispatcher will point you to the right crew.
